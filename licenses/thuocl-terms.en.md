# THUOCL terms: English reference translation

This is a translation of the [preserved upstream Chinese terms](thuocl-terms.txt), provided for reference. The original remains the source record; this translation grants no additional rights.

1. THUOCL is freely available to universities, research institutes, enterprises, organizations, and individuals worldwide, and may be used for research and commercial purposes.
2. Comments and suggestions about the toolkit are welcome. Send them to thunlp@gmail.com.
3. If you publish a paper or obtain research results based on THUOCL, acknowledge that you used the Tsinghua Open Chinese Lexicon when publishing the paper or reporting those results, and cite:

Shiyi Han, Yuhui Zhang, Yunshan Ma, Cunchao Tu, Zhipeng Guo, Zhiyuan Liu, Maosong Sun. THUOCL: Tsinghua Open Chinese Lexicon. 2016.

The original terms also provide the citation in Chinese.
